# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Prospect** — a home-service business owner (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, and similar
service-area trades) with no SEO knowledge. They land on the audit tool because they want to know
whether their website and Google presence are costing them leads. Their job to be done: submit their
business, wait through a short real-time process, and come away from the report with a clear,
non-technical understanding of what's wrong and what to do about it. Primary outcome: book a Local
Visibility Strategy Session.

**Cod3AI ops** — internal staff who review completed and in-progress audits via `/dashboard` and
`/admin`, qualify leads, and follow up. Their job to be done: quickly see audit status, scores, and
failures across all prospects without digging into raw data.

## Product Purpose

COD3AI S1T3SCOUT ("Local Authority Intelligence for Home Services" — tagline: *"Find the gaps. Own
the map."*) is a local SEO audit, scorecard, competitor benchmark, and action-plan tool built
specifically for home-service trades — not a generic SEO checker. It crawls a prospect's website,
cross-references their Google Business Profile, benchmarks them against local competitors, and
produces a scored, evidence-backed report with a 30/60/90-day action plan. Success looks like: a
non-technical business owner understands their gaps in minutes, trusts the findings because every one
is backed by a source, and converts into a booked strategy session or Cod3AI service opportunity.
Voice: a trusted local-growth strategist — direct, calm, specific, useful. State the evidence, the
business impact, and the next action; avoid hype, fear tactics, and dense technical language.

## Brand Personality

Dark, precise, high-trust, executive, conversion-oriented — a local-intelligence command center, not
a generic SaaS template or an "AI dashboard." See `DESIGN.md` for the full visual system (sourced
from the official brand kit).

## Anti-references

Purple gradients, glowing AI imagery, cartoon illustrations, fake charts, fake progress bars, and
over-animation. No hero-metric SaaS clichés, no gradient text, no glassmorphism-as-default, no
side-stripe borders, no tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section.

## Design Principles

- **Evidence over assertion.** Every score, finding, and competitor comparison must show its source
  (a crawled URL, an API field) — never a vague summary or an unbacked claim.
- **Real progress only.** Processing states reflect actually-persisted `audit_events` rows, never
  simulated timers or animations standing in for work that hasn't happened.
- **Non-expert clarity.** A home-service owner with zero SEO knowledge must be able to read the report
  and know exactly what to do next — plain language over jargon, always.
- **Honest about limits.** Unavailable data (no GBP match, no PageSpeed key, capped crawl) lowers
  confidence and is disclosed as a limitation — it is never silently scored as a failure or hidden.
- **Executive restraint.** Strong typography and generous spacing carry the premium feel; decoration
  (gradients, glow, illustration, motion) never substitutes for real information density done well.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline: minimum 4.5:1 contrast for body text (3:1 for large text), full keyboard
navigation, and `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for any future motion work. No unusual
accommodation needs called out beyond the AA baseline at this time.
